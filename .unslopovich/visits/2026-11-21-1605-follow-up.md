# Dr. Unslopovich — follow-up, four surfaces

```text
Verdict: Four fixes, and the screens now mean what they say. What they still don't do
         is tell a thumb where to go: the artist page hides two of its three panes
         behind a gesture nothing on the glass announces.
Reviewed: the treated screens only, from the committed renders and the code that
          draws them. Companion is from code; screenshots are still refused.
Before -> after (my re-score, same six categories, 4 each):
  firmware Music flow   13 -> 17 / 24
```

## Firmware Music flow — 13 → 17

| Category | Before | After | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Task path | 1 | 3 | Nothing blocks the path; the artist tab row is a real focus stop |
| Truth | 2 | 3 | Invented `OUT —`, duplicate mapping and `PLEASE WAIT` gone; stub launcher cells remain (the `--:--` placeholder was removed in #62, after this read) |
| Native fit | ~2 | 3 | One grammar for footers, one meaning per button, wheel enters and leaves the tab row |
| States | ~2 | 3 | `NO TRACKS IN LIBRARY`, `STOPPED AT THE START`, a cause sentence per stage |
| Access | 1 | 2 | Contrast fine; no text scaling by design; state no longer carried by colour |
| Specificity | 2 | 3 | The audio-pipeline readout and the serif carry identity |

**Still open, and it is the biggest thing on this surface:** the tab row is
reachable only by scrolling up past the first item, and neither the artist nor
the album page has a footer hint. The focus bar at x=16 is a 2×16 tick that
moves; a first-time user reads it as a caret if they see it at all. Three panes
sit behind a gesture the glass never mentions. Fixing it costs ~14 px of body
band, which the paper currently declares full: an owner layout decision, not a
patch.

Also open: `▶ PLAYING` in the now-playing state line reads as a transport control
on a device with a play button. The word carries the meaning; the glyph adds a
false affordance. Removing or replacing it is cosmetic but should be decided.

## Bootloader splash — 14 → 17

| Category | Before | After | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Task path | 3 | 3 | Unchanged: the splash is a status, not a task |
| Truth | 2 | 3 | The label is legible now; it still says the same words for pass and fail |
| Native fit | 3 | 3 | Owner artwork untouched |
| States | 2 | 3 | Three states, all readable |
| Access | 1 | 2 | 4.79:1 and 4.78:1, measured on RGB565 — but colour is the only cue |
| Specificity | 3 | 3 | The illustration is the identity |

**Still open:** pass and fail are the same two words in two hues at nearly the
same luminance, so a red/green colour-blind reader cannot tell them apart. The
words live in the owner mask. A shape or a word in the mask is the fix, and it
needs the owner's PNGs.

## What changed for the better, concretely

- 34 firmware PNGs re-rendered from the code the device runs, not hand-drawn.
- The four mockups of the same artist now agree with each other: same counts,
  same generated cover, and one no-art rule (monogram). The artists list used to
  give one artist a bust glyph and another a monogram, which read as favouritism
  and contradicted `docs/ui.md` 6.4.
- Long-name rows, empty states and the error screen were re-proved by pixel
  probes in `tests/ui`, so the next change cannot quietly undo them.

## Not checked

- No click wheel was used. The navigation is correct in code and pixel probes;
  whether a thumb finds the tab row is exactly the open question above.
- No companion screenshots and no device run anywhere.
- Frame time is still not profiled: the review is static, and the firmware
  repaints the whole panel per step.
