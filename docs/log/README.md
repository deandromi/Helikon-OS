# Work log

> Purpose: dated record of what was attempted, learned, rejected and what comes
> next. This is continuity material, not a second specification.
> Status: living document
> Owner: docs lead; session authors
> Last updated: 2026-11-03

## What belongs here

- One file per active day or session: `YYYY-MM-DD.md`.
- What changed, what was verified, what failed, what was learned and what was
  rejected.
- Links to commits, PRs, evidence and docs.
- Open questions that need an owner decision.

## What does not belong here

- Secrets, device backups, disk images, `/.rockpod-rw`, serial numbers, Wi-Fi
  credentials or personal data.
- A second copy of `state.md`, `tasks.md` or the design paper.
- Marketing claims or unrecorded measurements.

## Naming

`YYYY-MM-DD.md`, e.g. [`2026-11-03.md`](2026-11-03.md). If a day has multiple
sessions, append sections with a time/session label; do not create a second file
with a different scheme.

## Relationship to handoff

`docs/handoff.md` keeps the newest handoff at the top. When it grows, move older
entries here. A log entry can be more detailed than the handoff; the handoff is
the next-session summary.
