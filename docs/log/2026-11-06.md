## Native HelikonDB v1

Owner decision: **no iTunesDB anywhere**. HelikonDB is the only music catalog;
the Apple database is neither read nor written, and Apple-firmware library
parity is out of scope. D-07 and D-33 move to accepted with option (b), and
D-53 records the catalog ownership.

Implemented:

- `docs/helikondb.md` freezes the binary format: 128-byte header, 88-byte track
  records, 48-byte album/artist records, 24-byte playlists, a deduplicated
  UTF-8 string pool, four precomputed index arrays, 32-byte artwork records and
  96×96 RGB565 blobs.
- `tools/hdb_build.py` is the reference builder/inspector. It scans tags on the
  host, caches by mtime/size and by artwork SHA-1, deduplicates strings and
  artwork, and accepts optional track/album ratings, artist biographies and
  profile pictures. `fixture` emits a deterministic test DB; `inspect`
  validates the header, CRC and table bounds.
- `firmware/db/hdb.c` is the read-only firmware reader. It owns no buffers,
  takes a caller read callback, bounds-checks every table/string/blob access
  and optionally verifies the whole-file CRC.
- `tests/musicdb` generates the fixture, checks records, strings, indices,
  playlists, artwork CRC and malformed headers, and can open a real library.
