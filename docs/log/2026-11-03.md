## Learned

- The donor V3.2 is overlay-based and explicitly treats native HFS+ metadata as
  immutable. Helikon-OS is the opposite by design: native HFS+ writes through a
  transaction engine. This must be stated as a product boundary, not a
  contradiction (C-01).
- The paper's §25 has 17 open decisions; the task requires 20. Licensing, code
  of conduct and signing-key ownership were added from §18/§28.4.4 (C-08).
  Licensing (GPL-2.0-or-later) and code of conduct (Contributor Covenant 2.1)
  were accepted in the follow-up; signing-key ownership remains `proposed`.
- The README goals are broader than the paper's design response in skins,
  database timing, DAC scope, games/apps and device range. These are recorded as
  C-02–C-06, not silently narrowed.
- The donor's battery report is not Helikon-OS evidence and must not be reused.
